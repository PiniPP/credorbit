## When you need a commercial license

You need one if CredOrbit is used by or on behalf of a for-profit organisation, for example:

- employees or contractors using it to access the company's AWS accounts;
- running it on company laptops, servers, CI pipelines or other infrastructure;
- bundling it into, or offering it as part of, a product or service;
- evaluating it for company adoption beyond a short trial.

Using CredOrbit at work without a commercial license is not permitted by the
PolyForm Noncommercial license.

## What a commercial license includes

- the right to use CredOrbit across your organisation (per-seat or site licensing);
- permission to modify it for internal use;
- priority issue handling and security notifications;
- invoice-based billing.

## How to get one

Open an issue titled **"Commercial license request"** at
<https://github.com/PiniPP/credorbit/issues> with your organisation name and the
approximate number of users. You will be contacted privately to agree terms.

If you are unsure whether your use is commercial, ask first; it costs nothing to check.
